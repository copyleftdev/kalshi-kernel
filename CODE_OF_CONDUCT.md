# Community code of conduct

We want a technically rigorous community where people can participate without
harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or abuse.

Contributors must:

- discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics;
- assume good intent while remaining evidence-driven;
- accept respectful review and correction;
- protect credentials, private account information, and embargoed security
  reports;
- avoid manipulative promotion, financial promises, or claims of endorsement;
  and
- follow applicable laws, platform policies, and repository rules.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, market manipulation, and attempts to use the project for fraud
are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community or project. Report
ordinary conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when a report also contains a vulnerability or
sensitive information.
